5 Traps, caveats & extras

Everything that could change the totals or the buying decision.

⚠️

Unverified — confirm before buying

  • Canada shipping on every listing (needs postal code at checkout)
  • MakerBak3d thigh finish (photos look painted; description not fully read)
  • MakerBak3d knee: likely priced per single piece
  • Finished-helmet quality at both tiny shops (17 & 18 sales)
  • UA3D finished arm set: 3.8★ from 4 reviews
💾

Digital STL files (separate lane)

  • Helmet: US$1–15 · Chest: US$25 · Codpiece: US$6–8
  • Vambrace/gauntlet: US$11.42–12 · Knee: US$9.37–17
  • Needs your own printer + ~2–4 kg filament + full finishing
🥋

Not included: soft parts

Flight suit, flak vest, cape, neck seal, gloves, boots and belt are all required for a complete Din Djarin cosplay and were not researched — budget for them separately.
